Austera Transport Group (ATG) is a fictional organisation used for this portfolio project. AI use cases must be registered before implementation. Low-risk internal assistants may use a streamlined approval path when their data and impact remain limited. High-risk AI use cases require review by the AI Governance Committee following architecture, cybersecurity, privacy and risk assessments. Critical-risk AI use cases require escalation to the Executive Risk Authority. Approval is required before production use. The assigned risk tier must be reassessed when data sensitivity, user population, autonomy or operational impact changes.
